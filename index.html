01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Curious by nature.
Educator by purpose.

REBECCA L. JONES · EdD, CCC-SLP
✳

I believe meaningful learning happens when we make room for students to think, question, revise, and grow.

Rebecca L. Jones, EdD, CCC-SLP, is a speech-language pathologist, educator, and researcher at Oklahoma Baptist University. Her work focuses on the intersection of clinical education, artificial intelligence, and instructional design.

She explores how educators can develop clinical reasoning, create opportunities for meaningful practice, and use emerging technologies thoughtfully—without confusing a polished assignment with demonstrated clinical competence.

Her scholarly interests also include professional preparation, competency-based assessment, and the future of learning in communication sciences and disorders.
